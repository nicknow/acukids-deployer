Good questions

Before you begin.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. You’ll need to make an Ubuntu installer USB and follow its guided setup, then paste one command. Our detailed guide walks through every step.

Is acuKids really free?

Yes. acuKids is free, open-source software. Ubuntu is free too. Your only cost may be a used computer and an 8 GB USB drive.

Does it make the internet completely risk-free?

No technology can promise that. acuKids restricts the child’s Firefox browser to approved websites and adds family DNS filtering, while simplifying normal desktop access. Parents should still stay involved.

What is included?

Educational and creative apps, child accounts, PIN logins, screen-time limits, approved websites, automatic security updates, and a separate admin account for grown-ups.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Parents can add children, adjust time limits, and approve more apps or websites from the admin account.
